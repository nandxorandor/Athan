Privacy Policy — Athan (أذان)

Last updated: 28 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Athan mobile application ("the app", "we") handles your information. In short: Athan has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and collects no personal data. It makes exactly one kind of network request, described in section 2, and that can be switched off.

1. Location

The app uses your coordinates to calculate prayer times and the direction of the Qibla. You can either let the app read your device location once, or type a city name. Your coordinates are stored only on your device, in the app's own private storage.

Prayer times and the Qibla are calculated on your device from those coordinates. No location data is sent anywhere for that purpose, and the app works with no network connection at all.

2. Temperature (the only network request)

The app can show the current temperature where you are. This is the only feature that uses the internet. To provide it, your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo so it can return the weather for that location.

  • Nothing else is sent — no device identifier, no account, no name, nothing that identifies you.
  • Nothing is stored on any server by us. We operate no server.
  • The app asks you once, before any coordinate leaves your device, whether you want this feature.
  • You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Temperature. With it off, the app makes no network requests whatsoever.

Open-Meteo's handling of requests is governed by their own privacy policy at open-meteo.com/en/terms.

3. Audio files on your device

If you choose to use your own recording for the athan, the app asks for permission to read audio files so it can list the athan recordings you have downloaded. This reading happens entirely on your device. No files are uploaded or transmitted, and the app does not read anything else.

4. Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. We do not receive your prayer times, your location, your settings or any usage information.

5. Notifications, alarms and audio

The app schedules alarms so the athan sounds at the correct time, and shows a notification while it plays. These operate entirely on your device.

6. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
